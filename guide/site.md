# konta.md (the site)

Repository: `konta-www`.

## Layout rules

- **Text to the edge.** Every paragraph, lead, note, aside and list runs to the right edge of the
  content (`.shell`), like the headings and panels above it. No `max-width` in `ch` or `rem` on
  text, and no rule that puts each sentence on its own line. The owner, 2026-11-03: text that
  ended in the middle of the page (the founders' offer on the home page, the calendar's notes,
  the e-Factura page) read as unfinished. Two-column blocks keep their columns; the text fills
  its own.
